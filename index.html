Project Agreement & Scope Overview

This document outlines the general scope, responsibilities, and payment expectations for this website project.
Important: This document is not a legal contract. It is provided for clarity, transparency, and alignment prior to work beginning.

Project Purpose

The purpose of this project is to design and build a website that accurately reflects how the business operates today, improves clarity for customers, builds trust, and supports inquiries or bookings.

Existing online information may be outdated. All website content will be based on confirmed details provided by the client during the discovery and clarification process.

General Scope of Work

  • Website structure and layout planning
  • Service pages based on confirmed offerings
  • Clear explanation of policies, rules, and requirements
  • Intake and “how to get started” information
  • Photo placement and basic gallery sections if content is provided
  • Mobile-friendly layout and readable formatting
  • Contact page with a working form
  • Basic social media linking if desired

Items Not Included Unless Agreed Separately

  • Paid advertising or marketing management
  • Social media management
  • Legal, compliance, or regulatory consulting
  • Third-party booking platforms or subscription fees
  • Photography services unless specifically arranged
  • Unlimited revisions or major direction changes

Client Responsibilities

  • Confirm current services, hours, contact details, and rules
  • Provide accurate policies and intake requirements
  • Provide intake forms or confirm required wording
  • Supply photos, logos, and brand preferences if available
  • Review content and provide timely approvals

Payment Structure

  • 50% of the project cost is due upfront before work begins
  • 50% is due upon completion, prior to final launch or handoff
  • Work begins after the initial payment is received

Domain & Hosting

  • Domain registration and hosting fees are not included in the project price
  • If a new domain is required, the client pays the domain purchase fee
  • If an existing domain is owned, it may be connected or transferred
  • Any provider fees remain the client’s responsibility

Revisions & Scope Changes

  • Reasonable revisions are included
  • Major changes may require an additional quote
  • Scope changes will be discussed before proceeding

Ownership & Future Updates

  • After final payment, website files and content created for the project are provided to the client
  • Ongoing updates or maintenance can be handled separately if desired
