Project Agreement & Scope Overview

Scope, responsibilities, and payment expectations
Important: This document is not a legal contract. It is a written acknowledgment intended to prevent misunderstandings.

Project purpose

This project is intended to produce a website that accurately reflects how the business operates today, reduces customer confusion, and supports inquiries or bookings.

General scope

  • Website structure and layout planning
  • Service pages based on confirmed offerings
  • Policies and intake requirements clearly explained
  • Contact page with a working form
  • Mobile-friendly design

Client responsibilities

  • Provide accurate services, hours, rules, and policies
  • Provide forms, photos, and branding if available
  • Review and approve content in a timely manner

Payments

  • 50% due upfront before work begins
  • 50% due upon completion before launch or handoff

Domain and hosting

  • Domain and hosting fees are not included unless stated
  • Client is responsible for domain ownership and renewals

Revisions and ownership

  • Reasonable revisions included
  • Major scope changes may require additional quotes
  • Final files are provided after final payment
