Quick heads up

This walkthrough makes the project expectations clear before work begins. It is designed to be simple and low-friction.

Summary of how this project works

This is the short version. The full details are below if you want to expand them.

  • We confirm your current services, rules, hours, and intake process first.
  • The website is built based on confirmed details, not outdated online info.
  • Pricing is split: 50% to begin, 50% at completion before launch or handoff.
  • Domain and hosting costs are paid by the client unless otherwise agreed.
  • Reasonable revisions are included. Major additions or scope changes may be quoted separately.
Why we confirm details first

Kennel and service businesses change policies and hours over time. Building a site off outdated info causes confusion, refunds, and lost bookings. The discovery step prevents that and keeps the final site accurate.

What's included

This is the general scope for a typical small business website build. Specific pages and features can be adjusted after discovery.

Included in scope
  • Website structure and layout planning
  • Service pages (boarding, daycare, grooming, etc., as applicable)
  • Policies and requirements page (vaccines, spay/neuter, timing rules, etc.)
  • Clear intake flow (how to start, what forms are needed, what to bring, what to expect)
  • Photo placement and basic gallery sections if content is provided
  • Mobile-friendly design and readable content formatting
  • Contact page with a working form
  • Basic link-out to social media (Facebook, Instagram, etc.) if desired
Not included unless added later
  • Paid advertising management (Google Ads, Facebook Ads)
  • Ongoing social media management
  • Legal or compliance consulting
  • Third-party booking platforms and their subscription fees
  • Photography services (unless specifically arranged)
  • Unlimited revisions or major direction changes after approvals

Goal: reduce friction, answer customer concerns, show trust visually, and make it easy to book.

Client responsibilities

This part prevents the classic delays: missing info, missing photos, and unclear policies.

What the client provides
  • Confirmed services offered (what is active today)
  • Confirmed hours and contact details
  • Confirmed rules and requirements (vaccines, spay/neuter, intake timing)
  • Forms and intake documents (or confirmation of what they should say)
  • Photos, logo, and brand preferences if available
Approvals and responsiveness

The project moves fastest when feedback is given in batches and approvals are clear. If approvals are delayed, the timeline naturally extends.

Payments, domain, and updates

Payment structure
  • 50% upfront to begin work
  • 50% due upon completion, prior to final launch or handoff
  • Work begins after the initial payment is received
Domain and hosting
  • Domain registration and hosting fees are not included in the project price.
  • If a new domain is needed, the client pays the domain purchase fee.
  • If an existing domain is owned, it can be connected or transferred. Provider fees remain the client's responsibility.
  • DNS changes and connection setup are included as part of launch setup.
Revisions and scope changes
  • Reasonable revisions are included.
  • Major changes like new pages, new features, or big direction shifts may require an additional quote before proceeding.
Ownership and future updates
  • After final payment, the website files and content created for the project are provided to the client.
  • Ongoing updates and maintenance can be handled separately if desired.
